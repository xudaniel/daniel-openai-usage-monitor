## Usage Pulse

Usage Pulse reflects Daniel’s combination of product thinking, technical curiosity, privacy awareness, and interest in practical decision support.

The project was created to help users understand their available AI capacity, usage history, reset timing, and estimated runway without requesting passwords, session credentials, or unrelated personal information. It is designed to distinguish clearly between official information, locally stored readings, and calculated estimates.
